Axis 1 — Format Category (multi-value)
Standard Deluxe Limited Box Set Artbook Kanzenban Variant Omnibus

Flat chips. No foil, no animation. Kanzenban & Variant are formats — their chip color communicates type, not scarcity.

Axis 2 — Rarity Tier (single value, 3 levels)
◆ Rare Limited confirmed, no known print run. Gold border + frozen diagonal streak on cover.
◆◆ Super Rare 250–500 copies known. Gold border + lower-third warmth overlay. Shine peak 0.6.
✦ Ultra Rare <250 copies or manual holy grail. Full holo overlay moves with tilt. Shine peak 0.85.

Most items have NO rarity tier — no badge, no foil. The silence makes the rarity meaningful.

Single-tome vs Multi-tome — Stacked Card Effect

Multi-tome editions show 2 paper sheets behind the front card (surface-2/surface-3), offset 3.5px and 7px upper-right. Communicates "this is a series, not one volume". Foil only on the front card.
